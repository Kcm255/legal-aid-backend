You are further informed that you have the right to raise a
representation or objection to this notice, along with reasons,
within the notice period. The Bank is obligated to consider such
representation and communicate the reasons for non-acceptance, if any,
within 15 days.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY TERMS EXPLAINED (for reference/extraction):
- "NPA" — Non-Performing Asset: a loan account where repayment has
  been overdue for a specified period (commonly 90 days).
- "Section 13(2) Notice" — a mandatory 60-day demand notice a bank
  must issue before taking possession of secured property.
- "Section 13(4)" — allows the bank to take possession/sale of the
  secured asset if dues remain unpaid after the notice period.
- Borrower's remedy: an application (not technically an "appeal", but
  functions like one) lies before the Debts Recovery Tribunal (DRT)
  under Section 17 of the Act, within 45 days of the Section 13(4)
  enforcement action being taken.
